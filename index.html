Job application email kit

Version chuno, details bharo, Copy dabao, phir Gmail compose mein paste karo.

Har version apni position, skills, projects aur resume link yaad rakhta hai.

Is application ki details

Company name khali hai. Email mein [Company name] likha aayega, bhejne se pehle bhar lo.

Company aur greeting har bar naye bharne parte hain, taake purani company ka naam galti se na chala jaye. Resume file Gmail mein paperclip se attach hoti hai, yeh link sirf "View resume" button ke liye hai.

Skills (click karke on/off karo)

Jo skill off hogi woh email ki body, chips aur LinkedIn note mein nahi aayegi.

Projects (optional, max 3)

Jis project ka naam khali hoga woh email mein nahi aayega. Live Play Store ya App Store links sab se zyada kaam karte hain.

Gmail compose kholo, body mein click karo, phir Cmd+V (Windows: Ctrl+V).

Signature only

Permanent signature ke liye: Gmail Settings, See all settings, General, Signature mein paste karo.

Follow-up email (5 se 7 din baad)

Behtar hai ke follow-up pehli email ke jawab (Reply) mein bhejo, taake purani email aur "Re:" subject saath rahe.

LinkedIn connection note

LinkedIn ke connection note ki limit 300 characters hai, jahan tak mujhe pata hai. Bhejne se pehle LinkedIn par confirm kar lena.
